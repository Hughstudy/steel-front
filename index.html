STEEL FRONT · FIELD MANUAL 01

Know your ground.

Deploy the construction vehicle into a headquarters, then build a generator, barracks and motor pool from the production panel. Supply trucks haul crates from neutral dumps to your headquarters; engineers capture oil derricks for steady income. Buildings draw power: when demand exceeds supply, production runs at a quarter speed. In combat, AT guns and tanks kill armour, rifles and MGs cut down infantry and gun crews, artillery hits from long range, and fighters own the sky. Units you can't see stay hidden in the fog, and enemy structures you've scouted are remembered as last seen. Units gain veterancy from kills.
