# M4 — Combat and fog: verification

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Base commit:** `d6686dc` (M3)

## Scope and changed systems
- **Data:**
  - `data/weapons.json` (new): 20 weapons with small/HE/AP damage, penetration, range, minimum range, reload, projectile kind and speed, splash and falloff, target layers, aim tolerance, free aim, setup time and fire-while-moving.
  - `data/combat.json` (new): armour-class percentages, including the data-defined `gun` class for towed guns. Also minimum penetration, veterancy thresholds and bonuses, projectile pool size, acquisition and fog intervals, chase re-route interval and aircraft hold/altitude.
  - `units.json`: full roster per faction (rifle, MG, sniper, engineer, halftrack, light/medium/heavy tank, AT gun, howitzer, truck, fighter, MCV), with `armorValue` (mm) and turret turn rates.
  - `buildings.json`: Airfield (produces aircraft; heavy tanks need factory + airfield).
  - `production.json`: Aircraft tab (hotkey R).
  - `scenarios.json`: new default `m4-combat` ("Combat Sandbox": both bases and mixed armies) and `m4-stress` (100 v 100 mixed units). There is a per-scenario `fog` flag.
- **Simulation (all new modules are headless and deterministic):**
  - `spatial.ts`: uniform 8×8-tile grid for target queries.
  - `combat.ts`: acquisition, ordered attacks and chase, turret and hull aiming, setup time, cooldowns, integer damage model, splash, deaths in id order, kill credit and veterancy.
  - `projectiles.ts`: fixed-capacity struct-of-arrays pool. Slots are reused lowest-index first; a full pool delays fire and never drops damage.
  - `fog.ts`: per-player visible and explored masks, plus structure memory, recomputed every 4 ticks.
  - `air.ts`: fighters fly at constant speed with a limited turn rate and circle at their goal.
  - `world.ts`: `attack` command, accepted only against a currently visible enemy; per-tick `events` for presentation.
  - `rules.ts`: weapon/combat validation. Armour classes are now defined entirely in data, and the engine requires only `building`.
- **Presentation:**
  - `render/fog-layer.ts`: one shroud pixel per tile, drawn through the isometric affine transform on the battlefield and the minimap.
  - `render/effects.ts`: muzzle flashes, impacts, explosions, smoke, "PROMOTED" pops, interpolated projectiles and artillery arcs.
  - `entity-layer.ts`:
    - visibility filter;
    - memory ghosts;
    - aircraft drawn at altitude with ground shadows, and picked at altitude;
    - rank chevrons.
  - `main.ts`: a shared `tick()` feeds events to the effects; attack orders and a red crosshair cursor; minimap dots only for visible units and remembered ghosts.
  - Selection panel: VETERAN/ELITE and ATTACKING; ghosts show "LAST SEEN" with no live information.
- **Docs:** `BALANCE.md` covers the Spring: 1944 anchors, normalisation, substitutions and matchups. `docs/spring1944-source-notes.md` holds the raw source extraction.

## Design decisions
- **Damage:** `damage × class% × pen% × (100+vetDmg) × (100−vetArmor) / 1e8`, an integer with a minimum of 1 when the class can be hurt. AP penetration is `clamp(floor(pen²·100/armour²), 15, 100)`.
- **Auto-acquire:** units auto-acquire units only (never buildings). Ground units engage within weapon range; aircraft engage within vision. An ordered attack chases with re-routes rate-limited by `chaseRepathTicks`.
- **Information visibility:** one rule (`canSee`) applies to the main view, minimap, hover, selection panel, attack orders, health bars, projectiles and effects. Remembered structures are drawn as last seen, use negative ids, and never expose live HP or activity. Orders on unseen or remembered targets become plain moves, and the simulation independently rejects them.
- **Towed guns:** they are small crewed targets (`gun` class: small 50%, HE 100%, AP 15%). This keeps the counter loop: AT gun > tank, infantry/MG > AT gun, tank > AT gun only from outside the gun's range.

## Balance changes made during QA
- At first, towed AT guns used the `light` class and lost to the tanks they counter (PaK 40 vs Sherman: the gun died in 4.5 s). They now use the data-defined `gun` class (AP 15%).
- Coax MG damage was raised from 4 to 7 so tanks can fight unsupported infantry (Stuart vs 3 squads: win in 39 s).

## Not verified / known limitations
- No facing or flanking armour; one frontal value per unit.
- Idle units do not chase targets outside weapon range (no guard radius yet). The AI arrives in M5.
- Buildings are attacked only by explicit order; there is no auto-targeting of structures. Units out of range of an ordered building target close in.
- Real-time 60 Hz pacing over long sessions was not measured; benchmark and HUD counter only.
