# M5 — Skirmish AI: verification

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Base commit:** `e297edf` (M4)

## Scope and changed systems
- **Data:**
  - `data/ai.json` (new): per-faction plans (power/truck/engineer types, an ordered base plan with cumulative counts and optional tempo-scaled `afterTick`, and army composition weights).
  - Three difficulties: Recruit, Regular and Veteran. Each sets think interval, actions per think, tempo, truck and engineer targets, queue depth, army cap, first wave and wave interval, wave start/growth/max size, defence reserve/radius/calm time, power margin, placement tries, retry interval and bankruptcy retry interval.
  - `scenarios.json`: new default `m5-skirmish`. Each side starts with one MCV and 5000 credits; resources and fog are on; the Axis player has `"ai": "normal"`.
- **Simulation:**
  - `ai.ts` (new): a deterministic, tick-driven planner, one per computer player, running at the end of each tick. It only enqueues normal commands, which execute next tick through the same validation as the player's.
  - **Attack-move** (`amove` command, `Entity.amove`): units engage the nearest visible hostile within sight (units before structures), chase it, then resume toward the destination. Re-routing is rate-limited and the order ends on arrival. Plain move, stop and attack clear it.
  - `world.ts`:
    - `Player.start` holds the declared start sector.
    - `Player.ai` holds AI state as plain JSON data.
    - `loadScenario(scenario, { difficulty })`.
    - The AI runs after `flushRemovals`.
  - `rules.ts`: full `ai.json` validation (factions, unit roles, buildable structures, armed producible army, integer knobs, default difficulty) and the scenario `ai` field.
- **UI:**
  - Ctrl/⌘ + right-click attack-moves.
  - An ATK-MOVE selection button arms an attack-move click (red crosshair cursor).
  - An AI difficulty selector appears only for scenarios with a computer player and restarts the match.
  - The selection action row was compacted to fit 1024×768.
  - Development diagnostics come from `steelFront.ai()` (recent decisions and waves); they are never shown in the player HUD.

## How the AI decides (each think, under an action budget)
1. **Bootstrap:** deploy the MCV, or drive it to the nearest site where deployment is valid. The search is bounded and retried.
2. **Placement:** put a finished structure on the nearest valid site around the HQ that keeps a one-tile corridor around every structure. Up to `placementTries` candidates are checked per think, continuing from a cursor; after a full sweep it waits `retryTicks`.
3. **Bankruptcy:** if no income source exists and none is on its way, it frees credits for the truck and its prerequisite chain. It cancels queued non-essential items (full refund of what was paid) or sells one non-essential building, but only when that closes the gap. Otherwise it stalls for `bankruptcyRetryTicks`.
4. **Defence:** visible enemy units within `defenseRadius` of any owned structure draw every non-wave army unit on attack-move. Defenders return home after `defenseCalmTicks` of calm.
5. **Structures:** the first unmet base-plan step is queued, after any missing prerequisite (recovery). A power plant is queued first when power is low or the next building would breach `powerMargin`.
6. **Economy:**
   - Trucks are kept at `truckTarget`. A needed truck stuck behind army items brings itself forward by cancelling them (refunded).
   - Engineers are built only once hauling has started.
   - Engineers capture derricks closer to their own start than to the enemy's, never ones seen enemy-held or lost to the enemy.
7. **Army:** each non-structure queue is filled to `queueDepth` with the type of largest deficit against the composition weights. This pauses while the base or the first truck needs the money, and stops at `armyCap`.
8. **Waves:**
   - Each wave is drawn from units actually produced, nearest home first, keeping `defenseReserve` at home. It is delayed while a threat is recent.
   - Size is `min(max, start + growth × index)`.
   - The target is the nearest remembered hostile structure (fog memory); otherwise the enemy's declared start sector.
   - Idle waves push on to the next target, or disband and return home.
   - Launch tick, composition, size, target and arrival (a member within 12 tiles of the target) are recorded.

**Information rules:**
- The AI sees only through its own fog masks and memory.
- It knows the static positions of capturable map objects (map knowledge) and the enemy's start sector.
- It never reads hidden live units.

## Commands and results
- `npm test`: **69/69 pass**. New `tests/ai.test.ts` (9 tests):
  - **Legal opening:** both factions × all three difficulties, 9 minutes each. Credits never negative, every AI entity was paid for (the deployed HQ excepted), ledger exact, base plan through the motor pool, trucks and income, 3 or more armed units, power supply ≥ demand.
  - **Waves:** 3 or more escalating, mixed waves (3 or more unit types) against a passive base, each recorded as arriving after launch.
  - **Recovery:** removing a power plant, a truck and every barracks at 8 minutes. The barracks and power are rebuilt, trucks return to target, and infantry production resumes.
  - **Bankruptcy:** HQ + power, 0 credits, no trucks. The AI stalls with at most 2 commands in 10 minutes and the HQ is kept.
  - **Dump exhaustion:** emptying the two nearest dumps still yields deliveries, and further dumps are drawn down.
  - **Defence:** a raid of 2 rifle squads is engaged by at least 2 defenders and repelled. Trucks keep hauling, income continues and the base stays intact.
  - **Determinism:** identical replay of state, AI state and pending commands (Veteran, 7 minutes).
  - **Data-only difficulty:** editing `firstWaveTick`/`waveStartSize`/`defenseReserve` in the raw JSON changes the first wave's time and size.
  - **Attack-move:** it kills a bystander a plain move ignores, then completes the move.
- `npm run build`: pass (JS 192.4 kB, 60.4 kB gzip).

## Not verified / known limitations
- **No victory/defeat yet:** after the player's base falls, the AI keeps cycling waves to the empty sector. Results, menus and saves are M6.
- **Simple AI:** no scouting, retreat, focus fire or targeted counter-composition. Defence answers only threats near structures.
- **Map knowledge:** the AI knows where capturable derricks are and the enemy start sector (declared strategic knowledge); it does not track hidden units.
- **Attack-move and unarmed units:** an attack-move given to a mixed selection moves unarmed units like a plain move, which also stops a truck's hauling.
- **Not played by hand:** a full human-vs-AI match through to the end was not played in this session; the browser checks above cover the controls and AI behaviour.
