# M3 — Base building: verification

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Base commit:** `a5479bf` (M2)

## Scope and changed systems
- Data:
  - `data/production.json` (new): categories Structures/Infantry/Vehicles with hotkeys Q/W/E; queue limit 5; low-power rate 1/4; cancel refund 100% of paid; sell refund 50%; repair 1% HP every 10 ticks at 50% of cost per full repair; construction radius 7; placement timeout 200 ticks; default exit drive-out of 3 tiles.
  - `buildings.json`: per faction, Generator Station/Kraftwerk (+100 power), Infantry Barracks/Kaserne (−20, produces infantry, exit), Motor Pool/Panzerwerk (−30, produces vehicles, exit). HQ produces structures. New `produces` and `exit` fields.
  - `units.json`: real producers/prerequisites; MCVs with `deploy`.
  - `scenarios.json`: `m3-outpost` (one MCV, 5000 credits) is now the default. The M2 logistics fixture is kept.
- Simulation: `production.ts` (new) covers queues, progressive payment, power, prerequisites/producer checks, exits, pending placements/deploys with evacuation, sell, repair and rally. `world.ts` adds build/cancel/place/deploy/sell/repair/rally commands, power updates when buildings appear or disappear, and an extended ledger (`initial`, `refunded`, `sold`). `rules.ts` validates categories, producers, prerequisites (no dangling ids), exits and deploy targets. `truck-ai.ts` skips the route-field computation when no candidate exists.
- UI:
  - `ui/production-panel.ts` (tabs, cards with cost/progress/queue count/lock reason, keyboard context).
  - `ui/placement.ts` (ghost, deploy preview, sell/repair modes).
  - Buildings are selectable.
  - Right-click with producers sets the rally point, drawn as a dashed line.
  - Right-click on the selected MCV deploys; a DEPLOY button does the same.
  - POWER readout shows demand/supply (red when low), plus a scenario selector.

## Design decisions
- **Bootstrap:** MCV deploy has no credit or power prerequisite. The HQ footprint is centred on the MCV and validated with the shared rules (bounds, buildable, structures, access, no split); construction range is not required. On success the MCV is removed and the HQ placed in the same tick. A failed deploy keeps the MCV with status `deploy-blocked` and a HUD notice.
- **Tech tree (JSON):** HQ → power plant → barracks → motor pool. Infantry requires barracks; vehicles and trucks require the motor pool. The producer category is separate from prerequisites.
- **Queues:** one queue per category, and only the head item works. Work is an integer: `total = buildTicks × denominator`. Each tick adds `denominator` at full power or `numerator` on low power, so low power is exactly 1/4 speed. Cumulative cost due is `floor(cost × work / total)` and only the difference is charged. If credits are short, the item pauses with reason `funds` and makes no progress. Total charged always equals cost.
- **Placement:** the ghost and the `place` command both use `constructionProblem`. Units on a footprint are a soft problem: the building waits as a pending placement while those units are ordered to the nearest free tile outside it. Units are never deleted or teleported. The command revalidates at execution and on every pending tick. Invalid or stale placements keep the item `ready` and show a notice.
- **Exits:** finished units spawn on the producer's designated exit tile or the nearest free approach tile. If every exit is blocked, the item stays at the head with reason `exit-blocked`; it is not duplicated or charged again. When one producer is lost, others are used. Trucks start auto-hauling; other units drive to the rally point (or 3 tiles out of the door).
- **Prerequisite loss:** the head item pauses with reason `prerequisite`, makes no progress, and resumes when the building returns. New items cannot be queued without prerequisites.
- **Sell/repair:** sell refunds `floor(cost × 50% × hp/maxHp)` once (removal is idempotent). HQs are not sellable. Repair charges per step and waits when funds are short.
- **Input contexts:** the production panel (`tabindex=0`, `data-input-context="production"`) owns Q/W/E and digits while focused. Camera and battlefield handlers ignore events from it, held camera keys are released when focus enters it, and Esc returns focus to the map. Space on focused buttons still activates them (M0 rule).
- **Ledger:** `credits = initial + delivered + oil + refunded + sold − spent` is asserted in the tests.

## Commands and results
- `npm test`: **50/50 pass**. New `tests/production.test.ts` (11 tests):
  - starting credits vs the data-derived cheapest power → barracks → factory → truck path (4100, with a ≥15% buffer)
  - full bootstrap from one MCV to sustained truck income with an exact ledger
  - low power at 1/4 (measured tick ratio between 3.8 and 4.2)
  - funds pause/resume, with total charged equal to cost
  - cancelling partial, queued and ready items refunds exactly once
  - invalid, edge, water, out-of-range and stale placements never consume the item
  - squatters are moved, not deleted
  - blocked exit, prerequisite loss, producer handover and loss
  - MCV deploy failure, HQ not sellable, sell and repair
  - rally points (a water click resolves to reachable ground; HQ has no rally)
  - deterministic replay

  Also added: 20-unit crossing with a base on the route (movement) and hauling with buildings crowding the depot (economy).
- `npm run build`: pass (JS 135.6 kB, 44.2 kB gzip).
