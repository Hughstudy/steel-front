# M2 — Economy: verification

**Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Base commit:** `f0e4285` (M1)

## Scope and changed systems
- Data: `data/economy.json` (load/unload rates, dock lease, retry, oil interval/payout, capture duration), `data/buildings.json` (HQ fixtures with `depot`; neutral `supply_dump` with `supply: 3000`; neutral capturable `oil_derrick`), `data/maps.json` (Lowlands spawns, 10 dumps, 4 derricks), `units.json` (truck `cargo: 200`; Allied/Axis engineers with `capture`), `scenarios.json` (`m2-logistics`, plus `resources` flags).
- Simulation: `structures.ts` (footprints, ring/approach tiles, placement checks, split detection), `economy.ts` (dock leases, oil payout), `truck-ai.ts` (hauling state machine), `capture.ts` (engineer capture). `world.ts` gains buildings, removals at the end of the tick, a per-player ledger, and harvest/deposit/capture commands. `movement.ts` never shoves a truck that is docked and working, or an engineer that is capturing.
- Rendering: `render/building-atlas.ts` (procedural HQ, supply dump, derrick and future building sprites), `entity-layer.ts` (buildings depth-sorted by footprint, building picking/selection outline, a truck crate frame when loaded, cargo and capture progress bars).
- UI: `ui/orders.ts` (context right-click), FUNDS counter, an intel panel when clicking neutral/enemy structures, a cargo/task/status readout, harvest/capture cursors, scenario buttons and an AUTO HAUL command.

## Design decisions
- The HQ in `m2-logistics` is a **scenario fixture** placed at spawn 0, not built through construction (M3).
- Integer economy: dump stock, truck cargo and credits are integers. Stock drops only as cargo loads (10/tick). Credits rise only as cargo unloads (20/tick). The ledger records `delivered` and `oil`; the tests assert `credits == delivered + oil` and `initial stock == remaining stock + cargo in trucks + delivered`.
- Truck loop: `seek-dump → to-dump → load → seek-depot → to-depot → unload → repeat`. Targets are chosen by Dijkstra route cost to the cheapest approach tile, with id tie-breaks; a player-preferred target is tried first. Readable statuses: `no-resource` (stable retry; idles after 5 failures), `no-depot` (keeps cargo and retries), `queued` (all docks taken: parks 2–5 tiles away).
- Docks: each building has `dockSlots` leases `{truck, tile, until}`. A lease lasts 160 ticks plus 40 ticks for each tile of travel, and is renewed while working. Leases are purged when they expire, when the truck no longer targets the building, or when the truck is removed.
- A manual move cancels autonomy (`auto=false`, cargo kept). A harvest/deposit right-click or AUTO HAUL resumes it.
- Capture: the engineer must stand adjacent and stationary for 80 ticks. Any movement resets progress. On completion, ownership transfers, the engineer is removed once, and `timer=tick` stops a second engineer completing a capture on the same tick. Oil pays 25 credits every 100 ticks to whoever owns the derrick on that tick.

## Commands and results
- `npm test`: **37/37 pass** (28 from M1 + 9 economy tests: a five-minute ledger conservation run, dock slot limits and queueing, an exhausted dump then a stable no-resource state, no depot then a new depot with cargo retained, an approach blocker that is removed, a manual move suspending hauling and harvest resuming it, capture interrupted/consumed/paid, racing engineers, and identical repeated runs).
- `npm run build`: pass (JS 109.5 kB, 36.7 kB gzip).

## Browser QA (Claude desktop built-in browser, 1024×768 CSS, DPR 2)
The browser pane was hidden for most of the session, so `document.hidden` paused the game loop (the intended M1 behaviour). A QA hook `steelFront.advance(ticks)` steps the simulation synchronously and redraws. Pointer input was real clicks.
- **Dev (5173):** left-clicked the engineer, then right-clicked the oil derrick. A `capture:11` command was enqueued and the cursor showed the capture state. Left-clicked a truck and right-clicked the north supply dump: `harvest:1` was enqueued. After advancing 700 ticks the derrick belonged to player 0, the engineer had been consumed, FUNDS showed **1275** (ledger 1200 delivered + 75 oil), and the selected truck read `CARGO 200/200 · AUTO · hauling to depot` with its crate sprite showing. Left-clicking a supply dump with nothing selected showed `NEUTRAL · 2400 / 3000 supplies · docks 0/3`. Screenshot: `docs/m2-logistics.jpg`.
- **Preview (4173):** fresh load, capture command, then 6000 ticks (5 minutes) untouched: credits **9200** = delivered 7800 + oil 1400 exactly; the FUNDS DOM matched. Console: no messages.
- Render CPU benchmark (dev, 300 frames): median 0.1 ms, p95 0.2 ms.

## Not verified / known limitations
- Real-time 60 Hz frame pacing was not observed (pane hidden); CPU timings only.
- The derrick sprite does not visually distinguish owners beyond the HUD panel; team flags arrive with M3 building art.
